## Train the SGB regressor

### Concept: Training with Blindfolds
When we call `.fit()`, the sequential relay race begins just like before. Tree 1 tries to predict the bike rentals, calculates the Residual Errors, and passes those errors to Tree 2. 

The huge difference is that when Tree 2 is trying to predict those exact errors, the computer violently scrambles the data and the columns! Tree 2 only gets to see 90% of the rows and 75% of the features. 

When Tree 2 finishes, Tree 3 gets a *completely different* random 90% of rows and 75% of features!

Because the computer is doing significantly less math at every single step, this `.fit()` method finishes *much* faster than standard Gradient Boosting!
