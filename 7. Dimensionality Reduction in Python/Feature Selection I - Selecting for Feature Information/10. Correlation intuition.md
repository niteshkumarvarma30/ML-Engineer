## Correlation intuition

### Concept: Understanding Correlation
What statement on correlations is correct?

### Instructions

**Question:** Select the correct statement.
*   **The correlation coefficient of A to B is equal to that of B to A.** *(Correct)*
*   When two features have a correlation coefficient of 1 the values of both features are equal for each observation.
*   A correlation coefficient of 0 between two features A and B implies that high values of A are typically associated with low values of B.

*(This is because of the **Mirror Effect** we learned about! The top-right half of a correlation matrix is a perfect mirror image of the bottom-left half, because `A -> B` is mathematically identical to `B -> A`.)*
